# ANIMO-TB05A — Macropore Subsystem Bank Adoption

## Scope

TB05A is the first TB5 subsystem-bank adoption lane. It is limited to the macropore subsystem and does not modify production source, the central registry, TCD admission state, historical B2 evidence, or any whole-model golden baseline.

The permanent fragment keeps five claims separate:

- the MP01 activation/configuration and domain-ownership contract;
- MP02 complete-case B1 route reachability for active storage, exchange, Main Bypass drainage and C/N/P solute transport;
- the narrow STATEQ04 persistent-solute restart dependency;
- the still-open TCD-025 public/main ledger gap;
- the remaining historical-reference, full-restart, theory and numerical/build gaps.

## Evidence boundaries

MP01 is source/theory supported and diagnostically exercised, but it explicitly lacks qualified historical B2 reference evidence and is not B3-ready. MP02 advances this to complete-case B1 execution through B0-derived diagnostic descendants, but those descendants remain synthetic diagnostic evidence and are not behavioural goldens.

STATEQ04 is reused only as a narrow source-bound kernel restore contract. It is not a complete-model active macropore split-run qualification.

TCD-025 remains an open public/main ledger control-volume defect. Specialized water/solute route closure must not be interpreted as closure of that public ledger.

## GOV05 adversarial self-review

This is same-agent adversarial review and not independent. The review explicitly rejects seven overclaims: promoting B1 diagnostics to historical B2, promoting specialized closure to public-ledger closure, widening STATEQ04 to complete-model restart, implementing or admitting TCD-025, creating a behavioural golden, mutating the central registry, or introducing a new tolerance.

## Qualification meaning

Qualification of TB05A means the macropore subsystem evidence has been converted into a bounded, permanent testbank fragment with explicit negative scope. It does not qualify the subsystem for release and does not remove any blocker listed by MP01/MP02.
