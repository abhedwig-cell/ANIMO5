# Organic-P redistribution balance-bookkeeping defect

Status: `CONFIRMED_LEGACY_BALANCE_BOOKKEEPING_DEFECT_CAUSALLY_CORRECTED_IN_DIAGNOSTIC_PROBE`.

This note records PREP01 diagnostic evidence against the supplied revision-53 source and the deterministic LWKM testcase execution. The frozen source and testcase are unchanged.

## Observed balance envelope

The deterministic diagnostic balance envelope reports the largest organic-P period deviation in `LWKM_gras_1040.2021.2045`, `bapoGP.Out`:

- 1997: approximately `+0.0441 kg/ha P`;
- 1998: approximately `+0.0494 kg/ha P`;
- cumulative deviation later in the run: approximately `+0.154 kg/ha P`.

A direct `TRANSPORT` audit of soluble organic P for the 1998 balance period gives only about `1.45e-7 kg/ha P` net local transport nonclosure. The large `Bapo(Ddev)` value is therefore not generated by the soluble-organic-P transport solver.

## Source asymmetry in redistribution bookkeeping

`Addit.for` explicitly tracks the removal of dissolved organic P from the separate top reservoir during ploughing in:

`Addiorpotoppl(I) = - Codiropotop * Hetop`

and redistributes the combined material into the ploughed soil layers. It also records layer-level dissolved-organic-P changes in `Addiorpopl`.

`Outbal_calc.for` correctly uses the analogous top-reservoir terms for other conserved families:

- dissolved organic matter: `Addiormatoppl`;
- organic N: `Addiornitoppl`;
- inorganic P: `Adpotoppl`.

For organic P, however, `Bapo(Redi,Ly)` uses only `Addiorpopl(I,Ln)*Z` and never includes the already available `Addiorpotoppl(I)*Z` term.

This breaks the symmetry of the ledger. Material removed from the top reservoir and mixed into soil is counted as positive layer redistribution, but the corresponding negative top-reservoir transfer is omitted from `Bapo(Redi)`.

## Exact diagnostic reconciliation

Instrumentation over the deterministic LWKM execution gives, for every one of the three configured balance profiles:

- cumulative top-reservoir organic-P redistribution: `-0.15380316471773922 kg/ha P`;
- cumulative layer dissolved-organic-P redistribution: `+0.15380316471773947 kg/ha P`;
- cumulative stable-DOP redistribution contribution: `0.0 kg/ha P` in this case;
- conservative combined redistribution: approximately `2.50e-16 kg/ha P`.

The legacy organic-P balance records only the positive layer term. Its cumulative spurious redistribution input is therefore `+0.15380316471773947 kg/ha P`, which explains the approximately `+0.154 kg/ha P` cumulative balance deviation.

The same source structure explains why the visible period deviations are concentrated in years with ploughing redistribution and nearly vanish in intervening periods.

## Causal balance-only correction probe

A temporary diagnostic copy of `Outbal_calc.for` added exactly the already existing top-reservoir bookkeeping term when `Ln==0`:

```text
Bapo(Redi,Ly) = Bapo(Redi,Ly) + Addiorpotoppl(I)*Z
```

No model state, process rate, transport calculation or testcase input was changed.

For the LWKM GP balance the probe changes:

- 1997 period deviation: `+4.41e-2` -> `+5.07e-13 kg/ha P`;
- 1998 period deviation: `+4.94e-2` -> `+4.61e-10 kg/ha P`;
- final cumulative deviation in the diagnostic run: approximately `+1.54e-1` -> `+2.78e-8 kg/ha P`.

The largest remaining period deviation in the corrected diagnostic balance is about `4.06e-8 kg/ha P`.

Classification: `CONFIRMED_LEGACY_BALANCE_BOOKKEEPING_DEFECT`.

This is a ledger/output defect, not evidence that the underlying organic-P process physics or transport state changed by `0.154 kg/ha`. The state redistribution is already conservative in the measured top-plus-layer control volume; the legacy balance omitted one side of that internal transfer.

## Additional source observation

The revision-53 source also passes `AdStdiorpopl` and `Adhuexpopl` into `Outbal_calc`, while the organic-P redistribution block does not use those exact P-specific arrays in the same way that the organic-N block uses `AdStdiornipl` and the top-reservoir organic-N term. In the investigated LWKM case the measured stable-DOP net redistribution is zero, so this does not contribute to the demonstrated `0.154 kg/ha` defect. It remains an interface/ledger item to audit before a general corrected implementation is admitted.

## Qualification consequence

The organic-P residual envelope must not be used as a numerical acceptance tolerance. The dominant observed residual is a deterministic omission in balance bookkeeping and is removable without changing physics.

A corrected-legacy and ANIMO5 mass ledger should:

1. represent each conserved surface and soil store explicitly;
2. record both sides of internal redistribution transfers;
3. make internal transfers cancel exactly when both stores lie inside the selected control volume;
4. derive profile-boundary transfers from store ownership rather than hand-maintained parallel arrays;
5. test period and cumulative closure independently.
