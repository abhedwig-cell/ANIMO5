# TCD-016-C1 dry-down / rewetting discrimination experiment

Authority: ANIMO-SQ11, based on ANIMO-SQ10 at `e385f637c14b67f25670d7e9edd85d4aaefdb63b`.

## Purpose

SQ09 defined a bounded model-evolution candidate, `REWETTABLE_SURFACE_NH4_N_RESIDUE_EQUIVALENT`, but the Class-F theory/material-mapping and process-validation gates remained closed. SQ10 then showed that generic ammonium literature is scientifically useful but cannot identify the ANIMO continuation material or uniquely select complete instantaneous rewetting.

SQ11 therefore does not attempt another admission. It pre-registers an experiment and evidence interface capable of discriminating the candidate from the credible alternatives identified in SQ10.

A design is not evidence. No physical observations are generated by this repository workunit.

## Scientific question

After a traceable surface aqueous NH4-N pool dries until explicit aqueous surface storage is absent, what happens to the retained N when water returns?

The candidate predicts that, at the first experimentally resolvable post-rewetting state, all retained continuation N appears in the surface aqueous NH4-N receiver. A valid experiment must also be able to detect partial recovery, delayed recovery, transfer to soil/mineral storage, gaseous loss and residual non-aqueous material.

The word `instantaneous` is operational. It means the earliest defensible sampling resolution declared before execution. SQ11 does not invent an instrument-independent time threshold.

## Two-stage design

Stage A uses an inert, mass-accountable surface. Its purpose is to isolate residue formation and rewetting from soil/mineral retention. The study must record the NH4-N source and counter-ions, initial aqueous mass, dry endpoint, atmospheric NH3 loss or a defensible bound, residue composition/phase information, rewetting amount/composition, time-resolved aqueous NH4-N, terminal retained N, and total N mass closure.

Stage B repeats the sequence with a defined soil or mineral contact matrix. It adds a separately quantified soil/mineral NH4-N receiver. This stage is necessary because SQ10 found soil/mineral retention to be a credible competing fate, while also showing that such literature cannot simply be mapped onto TCD-016-C1.

A source-specific tracer, preferably `15N`-labelled ammonium or an analytically equivalent source tracer, is strongly recommended when background N would otherwise obscure source attribution. It is not declared mandatory because method choice belongs to the executing laboratory and must be justified by the actual matrix and detection system.

## Minimum observables

The evidence package must contain, or tightly bound where explicitly allowed, initial aqueous NH4-N, retained N at the dry endpoint, NH3-N loss during dry-down, residue material/phase characterization, a post-rewetting aqueous NH4-N time series, soil/mineral receiver N where relevant, NH3-N loss during rewetting, terminal non-aqueous retained N, and total mass-balance residual with uncertainty.

Metadata must include temperature, pH, ionic composition/counter-ions, surface material, soil/mineral material where present, initial water amount, drying method and endpoint, rewetting amount/composition/time, sample times, analytical LOD/LOQ and uncertainty, gas-capture recovery, and sample/batch identity.

## Pre-registered discrimination

No universal numerical epsilon is introduced. The executing study must define its analytical acceptance envelope from calibration, recovery, uncertainty, quantification limits and, where replicated, variance.

M1 can be supported only for the tested condition when first-resolvable aqueous recovery is compatible with complete recovery, no material delayed increase occurs, competing soil/mineral and gaseous terms are absent or bounded inside the declared analytical envelope, terminal non-aqueous residue is absent or similarly bounded, mass closure passes, and the tested residue has enough characterization to support a traceable material mapping.

M1 is refuted for a tested condition when a material terminal residue remains, a material delayed increase occurs, a material soil/mineral receiver is observed, a material rewetting gas loss occurs, or first-resolvable aqueous recovery remains materially below complete recovery after measured sinks are accounted for.

The result is inconclusive when mass closure fails, source N cannot be distinguished from background or contamination, residue characterization is insufficient, temporal resolution cannot distinguish instantaneous from finite-rate release, or an unmeasured pathway cannot be tightly bounded.

A positive result does not automatically establish a universal ANIMO constitutive law. Applicability remains limited to the material and environmental envelope actually tested until a separate mapping argument justifies extension.

## Replication and randomization

SQ11 does not invent a replicate count. The executing laboratory must predeclare replication, randomization or blocking from pilot variance, analytical precision and a stated power target or other defensible design criterion. This avoids treating an arbitrary sample count as scientific evidence.

## Evidence package and provenance

Raw observations must be immutable or referenced through a governance-approved immutable external location. Processing from raw observations to derived fractions must be reproducible. Protocol versions, calibration/QC records, sample lineage, source-material batch identity, laboratory/operator identity and dates must be retained. Negative and inconclusive runs may not be silently removed.

The machine-readable interface is `integration/animo-science/SQ11_TCD016_C1_EVIDENCE_PACKAGE_SCHEMA.json`.

## Qualification boundary

SQ11 can qualify the design as discriminating, pre-registered and fail-closed. It cannot qualify the M1 physics candidate because no physical dataset is executed or acquired here. The two SQ10 Class-F blockers therefore remain closed, TCD-016-C1 and parent TCD-016 remain not admitted, TCD-016-E1 remains blocked, and production remains untouched.

The next scientific step is external execution or acquisition of a traceable equivalent dataset. Only after such data exist should a separate evidence-ingestion and Class-F reassessment workunit be opened.
