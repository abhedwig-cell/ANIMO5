# TCD-016-C1 dry-down / rewetting discrimination experiment

Authority: ANIMO-SQ11, based on ANIMO-SQ10 at `e385f637c14b67f25670d7e9edd85d4aaefdb63b`.

## Purpose

SQ09 defined the bounded model-evolution candidate `REWETTABLE_SURFACE_NH4_N_RESIDUE_EQUIVALENT`, but the Class-F theory/material-mapping and process-validation gates remained closed. SQ10 showed that generic ammonium literature is useful but cannot identify the ANIMO continuation material or uniquely select complete instantaneous rewetting.

SQ11 does not attempt admission. It pre-registers an experiment and evidence interface able to test the specific continuation-to-aqueous transfer while preserving the SQ09 ordering contract.

A design is not evidence. No physical observations are generated by this repository workunit.

## Scientific question and ordering

After a traceable surface aqueous NH4-N pool dries until explicit aqueous surface storage is absent, does the retained continuation pool transfer completely to surface aqueous NH4-N when water returns?

The SQ09 candidate is an ordered transfer. It places continuation mass in `SURFACE_AQUEOUS_NH4_STORAGE` before downstream runoff, infiltration, sorption-after-arrival, transformation or atmospheric loss consume that aqueous mass. Therefore later soil/mineral uptake or atmospheric loss is not, by itself, evidence against M1. An experiment must separate the initial transfer from downstream fate.

The word `instantaneous` is operational. It means the earliest defensible sampling resolution declared before execution. SQ11 introduces no instrument-independent time threshold.

## Two-stage design

Stage A is the primary transfer test. It uses an inert, mass-accountable surface and suppresses or tightly bounds downstream sinks so that first-resolvable aqueous arrival can be attributed to rewetting of the retained residue. The study records source/counter-ion identity, initial aqueous N, dry endpoint, dry-down atmospheric loss, residue characterization, rewetting amount/composition, the earliest technically defensible aqueous sample, a subsequent aqueous time series, terminal retained N, and full N mass closure.

Stage B uses a defined soil or mineral contact matrix. Its purpose is different: assess ordering and downstream fate under representative contact. A soil/mineral receiver is not automatically a competing first receiver. It refutes M1 only if source- and time-resolved evidence is strong enough to show direct alternative transfer without prior surface-aqueous arrival. Soil uptake after demonstrated aqueous arrival remains compatible with the SQ09 contract.

A source-specific tracer, preferably `15N`-labelled ammonium or an analytically equivalent source tracer, is strongly recommended when background N would otherwise obscure source attribution. It is not mandatory because method selection belongs to the executing laboratory and must be justified by the actual matrix and analytical system.

## Minimum observables

The evidence package must contain, or tightly bound where explicitly allowed, initial aqueous NH4-N, retained N at the dry endpoint, atmospheric N loss during dry-down, residue material/phase characterization, a post-rewetting surface-aqueous NH4-N time series beginning at the earliest defensible sample, soil/mineral receiver N where relevant, atmospheric loss during rewetting where relevant, terminal non-aqueous retained N, and total mass-balance residual with uncertainty.

Metadata include temperature, pH, ionic composition/counter-ions, surface material, soil/mineral material where present, initial water amount, drying method and endpoint, rewetting amount/composition/time, sample times, analytical LOD/LOQ and uncertainty, downstream-receiver/loss measurement methods, and sample/batch identity.

## Pre-registered discrimination

No universal numerical epsilon is introduced. The executing study defines its analytical acceptance envelope from calibration, recovery, uncertainty, quantification limits and, where replicated, variance.

M1 can be supported for a tested condition when Stage A first-resolvable source-attributable aqueous recovery is compatible with complete transfer after accounting for any independently measured downstream loss occurring before sampling, no material delayed release is observed, terminal non-aqueous residue is absent or bounded within the analytical envelope, mass closure passes, and residue characterization supports a traceable mapping.

M1 is refuted for a tested condition when material non-aqueous residue remains after rewetting under a closed mass balance, source-attributable aqueous recovery materially increases after first activation because of delayed release, a direct alternative receiver is demonstrated with sufficient temporal/source evidence to exclude prior aqueous arrival, or first-resolvable aqueous arrival plus independently measured downstream transfer before sampling remains materially below retained continuation mass.

Soil/mineral uptake, atmospheric loss, runoff, infiltration, sorption or transformation occurring after demonstrated surface-aqueous arrival are compatible with M1 and must not be misclassified as failure of the rewetting transfer law.

The result is inconclusive when mass closure fails, source N cannot be distinguished from background/contamination, residue characterization is insufficient, temporal resolution cannot distinguish complete transfer followed by downstream loss from direct alternative transfer or finite-rate release, or an unmeasured pathway cannot be tightly bounded.

A positive result remains limited to the tested material and environmental envelope. It does not automatically establish a universal ANIMO constitutive law.

## Replication and randomization

SQ11 does not invent a replicate count. The executing laboratory predeclares replication, randomization or blocking from pilot variance, analytical precision and a stated power target or another defensible design criterion.

## Evidence package and provenance

Raw observations must be immutable or referenced through a governance-approved immutable external location. Processing from raw observations to derived quantities must be reproducible. Protocol versions, calibration/QC records, sample lineage, source-material batch identity, laboratory/operator identity and dates are retained. Negative and inconclusive runs may not be silently removed.
