# ANIMO-B3I10 — TCD-042 positive-HETOP child-scope routing

This workunit performs scope governance and routing only. It performs no scientific admission, no production implementation, no input-policy change and no new top-level TCD reservation.

B3I05 atomized TCD-042 into B1 exact-zero upper-reservoir algebra and E1 finite-positive subthreshold numerical policy. Subsequent B3I06 and UBQ04 established that revision-53 accepts `HETOP=0`, but does not uniquely define the model-level concentration semantics of that zero-thickness endpoint. B3I06 therefore classified the endpoint as a domain constraint, not a new child, and recommended keeping B1 and E1 bounded to `Hetop>0` pending authoritative endpoint semantics.

B3I10 makes that revision-53 scope boundary explicit. For frozen revision 53, `HETOP=0` is excluded from the supported B3/B4 child scopes. This is not a claim that zero is globally invalid input, and it does not introduce a sentinel, epsilon, guard or replacement physical model. A future model-evolution workunit may reopen the endpoint only with an explicit scientifically owned zero-thickness contract.

The routed B1 candidate is therefore `Flpn=0 AND Flux=0 AND Hetop>0`. Its positive-HETOP exact limit remains the B3B02 identity and may proceed to a separate admission decision after this routing closes. E1 remains a separate child and retains its own NQ03/NQ03R positive-HETOP and P-envelope restrictions. Parent TCD-042 remains unadmitted.
